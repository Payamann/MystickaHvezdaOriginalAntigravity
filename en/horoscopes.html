How to read your horoscope

A sun-sign horoscope is a general text. Choose a daily, weekly or monthly outlook and notice only the themes that are useful for your situation.

Instead of looking for a certain prediction, you might take one question from the text: where do I need more time, what would I like to clarify, or what matters today?

For planet placements at the time of your birth, try the English birth-chart calculator. The tarot and angel-card guides clearly mark links to Czech readings.

Common questions

Questions about horoscopes

Are the horoscopes free?

The daily horoscope is free without registration. Further use may be subject to limits. Accounts, pricing and paid services are currently in Czech.

How is the horoscope created?

The text is generated automatically for the selected sign and period using astrological themes. It is not a personal calculation based on your birth time and place.

Can I also read for my rising sign?

If you know your ascendant, you can also read the text for that sign. This offers another symbolic perspective, rather than a more accurate prediction of events.

What if the horoscope does not fit?

You do not have to apply it to your life. A general text for a zodiac sign cannot account for your personal circumstances or decide for you.
