How online tarot works

Tarot draws on images from the Major and Minor Arcana. The spread determines the number of positions and themes, rather than the reliability of a prediction. A symbol can open up your own thoughts about a question.

One card offers a single theme. Three cards can distinguish past experience, the present situation and a next step. The ten positions of the Celtic Cross offer more context without promising a certain outcome.

Common questions

Questions about tarot

Is online tarot free?

The Czech card of the day is free without registration. Full multi-card readings are part of paid membership. Readings, accounts and pricing are currently in Czech.

How should I phrase my question?

Focus on a step you can take and what you can influence. For example: what should I consider before having this conversation? A specific question helps reflection but does not guarantee an accurate reading.

Can tarot predict the future?

Not reliably. Cards offer symbols and themes to reflect on; they cannot confirm future events or another person's feelings. The decision stays with you.

What if a card means nothing to me?

You do not need to fit its meaning to your life. Write down your own thought or leave the card aside. Drawing again does not guarantee a better answer.
