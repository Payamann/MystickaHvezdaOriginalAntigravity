Common questions

Questions about angel cards

Is the angel card of the day free?

The basic draw and short message are free without registration on the Czech page. This English guide links to that page.

How do angel cards differ from tarot?

Angel cards use their own images and short encouraging themes. Tarot uses a different system of symbols and spreads. Both can support reflection without making decisions for you.

Do I have to draw a card every day?

No. You can return to a card when its theme feels useful. A daily ritual is an option, not an obligation.

What if the card does not fit?

You do not have to look for a hidden message or wait for it to come true. You can leave it aside and focus on what you need now.
