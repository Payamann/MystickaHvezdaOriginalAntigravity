What this birth chart shows

A birth chart uses your birth date, time and place. This calculator displays the signs and degrees of the Sun, Moon and other planets, plus the ascendant when sufficient information is available.

You can leave the time empty. Some placements will then be approximate. Even when you enter a time, the result also depends on the birthplace being recognised correctly.

The result is a short placement summary. It does not include a personal interpretation of your character, relationships or future. You can use astrological symbolism as a subject for your own learning and reflection.

Common questions

Questions about your birth chart

What details do I need?

Birth date and place are required; add the time if you know it. The accuracy of the time and recognition of the place affect the ascendant calculation.

Is it useful without an exact birth time?

You can still view approximate planet placements. Without an exact time, some details may be inaccurate, especially the Moon and ascendant. Keep this in mind when reading the result.

Is the birth-chart calculation free?

The basic placements are free to view here. This page provides a placement summary, rather than a detailed personal interpretation. Accounts, pricing and paid services remain in Czech.

What will the result show?

The signs and degrees of the Sun, Moon and other planets. The ascendant appears when it can be determined from your details. The result is not a prediction of your future.
